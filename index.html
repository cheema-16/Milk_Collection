Farmers Payment

Farmer Milk (L) Amount Advance Net Pay
Amount, Advance and Net Pay are in Rs. Amount = milk collected × the farmer's rate from Rate Management. Advance = the farmer's full advance balance. Net Pay = Amount − Advance (never below 0). If the advance is larger than the payment, the remainder carries over to next week.
